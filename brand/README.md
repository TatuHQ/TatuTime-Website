Interim source assets for the TatuHQ identity. The website uses optimised copies under
`site/assets/brand/`:

- `tatu-mark-original.png` — the pixel-art tatu mark at its native 420×420 on its original light
  background, taken from the TatuHQ organisation avatar.
- `site/assets/brand/tatu.png` — the same mark with the flat background removed and cropped, used in
  the navigation, hero, footer and 404 page.
- `site/assets/brand/tatuhq.png` — the company mark used in the “Built in the open” section.
- `site/assets/brand/favicon-16x16.png`, `favicon-32x32.png` and `site/favicon.ico` — generated from
  the same mark.

The mark is pixel art. Always scale it with `image-rendering: pixelated`; do not smooth, recolour,
crop or add filters to it (Firefox renders a white box behind transformed, filtered pixel art).

The former toucan lockup and its Illustrator source were retired with the TatuHQ rename. They remain
available in the repository history if the original artwork is ever needed.
